A LITTLE LESS BUSYWORK

BK Tools

Six independent browser tools, designed by Alireza Rajabi, with the familiar navy, gold and teal of Bizkonic.

Images, form inputs and data files are processed on your device. No account, analytics, upload endpoint or external processing service is used. Your inputs stay in memory while this page is open.

Browser clipboard, image encoding, downloads and email-client behavior can vary. Use the previews and keep the output that works for you.

Original BK artwork is preserved; this app uses its own BK + Tools icon. Bizkonic, Command Desk and Field Report remain separate apps.
